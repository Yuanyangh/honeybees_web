` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` `

Seeing your mother’s coin pouch,
you realise you’ll need a way to make money. ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` What’s your plan?
` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` `
While exploring the town,
you come across an orphanage looking for volunteers. ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` What service do you provide?
` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` `

` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` `

town celebrates during the orphanage’s festival. However, monsters controlled by the governor soon attack. Panic erupts. ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` What do you focus on doing?
` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` `

` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` `

you weigh the pros and cons of both solutions. ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` How do you do that?
` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` `
You know it’s a tricky situation either way because people will get hurt.

` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` ` `
